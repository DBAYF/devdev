Every BLOODVOLT chassis branches four times during a run:

- **L10** — first promotion. 4 base classes per chassis (24 across the roster).
- **L20** — sub-path. 3 children per L10 (72 entries).
- **L30** — refinement. 2 children per L20 (96 entries).
- **L45** — endgame terminal. 4 children per L30 (384 entries; 192 with extras).

In a single match the player reaches L30. The L45 selection is configured before drop
via the **CLASS TREE** menu — pick once, save as a build, share the 8-character
`BLDVLT:XXXXXXXX` code with friends. They can paste it on their main menu inbox or
launch it directly from the [game page](/games/bloodvolt) on devdev.

The Apr-26 balance pass also tightened L10 differentiation. WRAITH ASSASSIN now lands
at `dmg 1.85, crit 1.6` instead of `1.6 / 1.3` — a one-shot hunter is now an actual
one-shot hunter. KHAOS SIEGE got a `hpMul 1.65` bump. RAZOR BUTCHER's saw drops to
`fireRate 0.75` to make the heavy-hit identity clearer.
